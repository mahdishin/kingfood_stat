Ghormeh Sabzi (قورمه سبزى]-vegetarian

★★★★★ 0 · 0 reviews
$14.98

Ghormeh Sabzi is a classic Persian herb stew, slow-cooked for rich flavor and aroma. Made with fresh herbs (parsley, cilantro, chives, and fenugreek], red kidney beans, driedlime, and onions, it offers a savory, tangy, and comforting taste. Served with steamed rice.
